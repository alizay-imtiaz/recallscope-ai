A recall notice is easy to read. Tracing it is not.

A supplier announces a recalled batch. The team now has to find out whether it ever reached their business, and the proof is rarely in one place.

Scattered records

Purchase orders, inventory files, dispatch notes and the supplier notice sit in different folders and formats.

Inconsistent identifiers

B-2291-07, B-2291 and B2291/07 may or may not be the same batch. Someone has to judge.

Hard to audit

A conclusion with no links to its source documents is difficult for a colleague to check later.

Silent gaps

A dispatch note with no batch field is easy to overlook, and easy to mistake for “not affected”.

From scattered files to an evidence trail

RecallScope AI is designed around one question: where does this batch appear in our records? It is not a generic document summarizer. Every finding carries a category and a source reference, and what could not be established is listed explicitly.

Intended audiences: food distributors, wholesalers, inventory managers and operations teams. It is not deployed with any organization.

What a reviewer gets

  • Six match categories that keep confirmed and uncertain results apart
  • Links from each finding back to its source record
  • Explicit evidence gaps and conflicts
  • A report that states a human must decide

How it works

Seven intended stages, from notice to report. This is the designed workflow; Testing shows what has been validated.

Design intention

  1. Select a recall notice

    Pick the supplier notice to investigate.

  2. Choose authorized records

    Limit the search to folders the team approves.

  3. Extract identifiers

    Product names, supplier details, batch and lot numbers.

  4. Compare across documents

    Match identifiers against purchase, inventory and dispatch records.

  5. Connect evidence

    Link related purchase and dispatch records.

  6. Classify and flag gaps

    Assign a match category and list missing information.

  7. Generate the report

    Produce an impact report for human review.

Scenarios supported

  • Exact batch match
  • Partial or truncated batch
  • Product-level match, no batch field
  • Conflicting supplier, SKU or batch
  • Incomplete records
  • No match in searched scope
  • Edge: duplicate records
  • Edge: different batches of one product
  • Edge: dispatch dated before receipt

Known limitations

  • All data is fictional and built into the page; nothing is read from a NAS.
  • Folder selection is simulated; no AI extraction runs.
  • Matching rules are simple and conservative: normalised text comparison, one-character or truncated-prefix similarity.
  • Quantity conflicts are not checked; only dates.
  • State lives in memory and resets on reload.
  • PNG export is not available.
  • No match in scope never proves a product is unaffected; people make every decision.

Not claimed anywhere on this site: accuracy figures, time or cost savings, performance results, UGREEN endorsement. The synthetic scenarios are demonstrations, not test evidence from a NAS.

Responsible AI and limitations

RecallScope AI supports an investigation. It does not replace the people responsible for it.

  • Human reviewers make the final decisions.
  • It does not make final product-safety decisions.
  • It does not automatically change inventory.
  • It does not automatically notify customers.
  • Results depend on the authorized records selected.
  • A missing match does not prove a product is unaffected.
  • Partial matches must never be presented as confirmed matches.
